# Echoes Unseen 2.0 — a new chapter

Echoes Unseen is a free, unofficial Windows companion for Guild Wars 2, designed especially for players with low vision. This public beta brings the dragon launcher, creator trails, clearer menus, local dictation and a rebuilt manual Music Guide.

## Download and start

![Illustrated V2 layout showing the dragon launcher, compact tool menu and Sonar Trails panel.](https://github.com/Quinn80/EchoesUnseen/releases/download/2.0/Echoes-Unseen-V2-interface-preview.png)

*Interface mockup, not an in-game screenshot.*

Download **Echoes Unseen.exe** from the assets below. Windows 10/11, 64-bit; Guild Wars 2 required. This is a self-contained executable with no separate .NET installation. Close an older running copy before launching the new one, and back up your settings before upgrading.

Open Guild Wars 2, launch Echoes Unseen normally, then click the dragon or press **Alt+Down**. Choose a comfortable voice and text size in Settings. No website account or payment is required.

## What changed since 1.6B

- **Dragon launcher:** replaces the radial wheel with a movable dragon icon and a simpler tool menu. Hovering does not open it.
- **Clearer panels:** Account Search, Trading Post and Build & Gear have more readable layouts. Six tools separate everyday actions from their Settings view. Descriptions are shorter and reflect what the app actually does.
- **Creator trails:** GPU rendering, themed Ribbon + Arrows or Arrows Only, shifting galaxy effects, clearer objective markers and current-map guide browsing. Creator sections and gaps are preserved; Echoes does not invent connections between them.
- **Heart Quests:** Standard and Clarity presentation choices, optional objective speech, Repeat, and a 2D area preview where usable API boundaries exist.
- **Reading:** 400 ms default hover dwell, refinements for player names, Friends rows, trophies and mastery-rank context. Actual speech also includes OCR and synthesis time.
- **Account tools:** broader item search with locations, character equipment information and improved Trading Post search/results.
- **Communication:** chat-channel announcements, selectable whisper cues, separate voice choices and local Whisper dictation. A movable, resizable recorder has Copy, Read and Delete controls. Windows Speech Recognition is no longer the dictation engine.
- **Music Guide:** manual playing with ten aligned lanes, five themes, Practice / Original / Challenge / Custom pacing, local imports and source/timing labels. Timed arrangements use falling cues; untimed tabs stay static. Autoplay and the AutoHotkey engine have been removed.
- **Possible rift sites:** available in Sonar Trails, explicitly labelled possible rather than live.
- **Diagnostics:** reports identify the shown creator guide and rendering state more accurately.

Your choice of speech provider remains yours. Cori is the default app voice, and routing app speech through NVDA is optional.

## Known limitations

- OCR remains beta and may misread or miss text. Chat and whisper messages, especially wrapped lines, can still be read incompletely.
- Trails follow creator-authored data. They do not calculate a safe route around terrain, guarantee mount/mastery compatibility or identify active rifts.
- The Heart Quests area preview is inside the panel, not a ground-following world boundary.
- Some bundled music is an excerpt. Imported songs vary in timing and instrument range. Echoes does not play the instrument for you.
- This executable is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may warn; verify the download source and checksum rather than disabling Windows security.
- A MumbleLink startup failure with repeated error dialogs was observed in a DEV run of the same source. The release executable worked in Quinn’s test, but that does not establish that the failure cannot occur elsewhere. Recovery improvements are being tested separately in DEV. If affected, close Echoes with Ctrl+F12 and report the circumstances.

**Story Guide and scanner-based rift assistance are not included in this public build.** They remain in development. The .NET 10 migration follows this release.
